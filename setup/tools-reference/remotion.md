# Remotion

Draws motion graphics and animated video from the templates in `tools/motion/`, rendered on this computer with a headless Chrome. The `motion-video` skill drives it.

```
node tools/motion/render.mjs --composition CoverFrame --props card.json --out card.mp4 --aspect 9:16
node tools/motion/render.mjs --help
```

**Gotcha:** Remotion is free only for individuals, companies of three people or fewer, and non-profits; a bigger company needs a Remotion company license.
