# Stop starting over with your AI. One free prompt gives it a place to keep your work.

Paste this into an AI service that can run programs and keep files. It asks what you do, then sets up a folder for your work, your rules and the way you write. It asks before it sends, spends or deletes. Free.

```prompt
Download https://github.com/six20am/workspace-blueprint/archive/refs/heads/main.zip, unzip it into a folder of your own (not the shared root of your file area), read ADAPT.md in it, and follow it step by step. Ask me the setup questions when it tells you to.
```

## Seven reasons a folder beats a chat box

- **It keeps what you tell it.** Your files and your rules stay in one folder between chats. You stop pasting the same background in.
- **It works from your real files.** Drop in the PDF, the bank export, the three emails you wrote. It reads them where they sit.
- **It sounds like you.** It builds a voice file from your own writing, so the email it drafts could have come from your desk.
- **It already knows how to do thirty-nine things.** Transcribe a video, explain a bill, sort your photos, turn bookings into one trip plan, draft the post. The full list is further down.
- **It asks before it sends, spends or deletes.** It says so at setup, and you can add to that list. That rule holds in every chat after.
- **It tells you when it can't.** Ask for something it has no tool for and it says so, then offers to build one. It doesn't fake it.
- **The setup is free, and it all sits in one folder.** The code is public, so read it first if you like. Delete the folder and what it built is gone.

## Copy, paste, answer

1. **Copy the prompt.** Press any Copy the prompt button on this page.
2. **Paste it into your AI.** Not sure yours can do this? Ask it: "Can you run programs, keep files between chats, and keep a standing instruction?" If it says yes, paste. Your AI downloads the setup files and gets itself ready.
3. **Answer a few questions.** It asks what you do, then names the kinds of work it would set up and asks if that's right. It asks how it should sound. It shows you the folders and names what it will never do without asking: send, spend, delete. You can add to that list. "Not sure" is a fine answer to any of them. Then it builds the folders and tells you what to try first.

If your AI can only chat, this won't work. Use Claude Code instead. It's further down this page.

## Want it all on your own computer? Use Claude Code

Claude Code is Claude's tool for working on your own machine. You need a Claude account. Download the zip and everything installs on your computer. Windows is tested on one machine. Mac is new and untested.

1. **Unzip the download.** Move the folder to Documents.
2. **Run setup.** Double-click `setup.cmd` on Windows or `setup.command` on a Mac. A black window installs the tools and Claude Code. Fifteen to thirty minutes. Windows may ask "allow this app to make changes" a few times. Say yes. A Mac refuses it the first time: open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, and click Open Anyway.
3. **Answer a few questions.** Claude opens and asks them one at a time, then builds the folders.

## Seven things to try in your first week

Say what you want, name the file or link, and say where the result should go. Stuck? "What can you do with this?" is a fine first line.

3. Ask what people have been saying about something in the last 30 days.
4. Give it a website and ask for every photo on it.
5. Paste three emails you wrote and ask it to learn your voice.
6. Hand it three clips and ask for one video with a fade at the end.
7. Ask for something it has no tool for, and watch it say so and offer to build one.

Pick the one you'd do today. Then start here.

## What the zip installs. What setup sends out: nothing

Free, standard tools: Git, Python, Node.js, ffmpeg, a browser engine, Claude Code, and the readers and converters the skills use. The largest, LibreOffice, is 300 MB and more. <!-- faq -->
## Questions people ask

**Is it free?**
Yes. The blueprint is free and the code is public. AI services have their own plans, and those are between you and them.

**Do I need an account?**
Yes, with whichever AI service you use. The blueprint itself needs nothing. On Claude Code, Claude opens your browser to sign in the first time it runs.

**Where do my files live?**
On the computer your AI service gives it, under that service's rules. If you want them on your own machine, use Claude Code.

**What does it send out?**
On an AI service, your chats and files go through that service. On Claude Code, setup sends nothing. It signs in to nothing for you; the five sign-ins are yours to do, and setup lists them.

**Can I undo it?**
Yes. Delete the folder and everything it built is gone. On an AI service, also ask it to remove the programs it installed and the instruction it saved. On Claude Code, the programs uninstall the normal way: with the app installer on Windows (winget) or Homebrew on a Mac.
