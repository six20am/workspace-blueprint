# Skills index

One line per skill, from its description. Generated by the build; do not edit by hand.

- `agent-reach`: Use to READ or SEARCH content on platforms that a normal fetch can't reach, X/Twitter, Reddit, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, GitHub code search, and RSS feeds: plus any single web URL that WebFetch fails on (e.g. 402/403, login walls).
- `agent-web-fetch`: Governance and routing layer over the vendor agent-reach skill for reading gated or blocked web content: X/Twitter, Reddit, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, GitHub, RSS, and any URL that WebFetch refuses.
- `channel-mine`: Mine an entire YouTube channel, playlist or podcast back catalogue for everything useful, and file it as a source extract.
- `chrome-tools`: Which browser tool to use for a page behind a login: the Claude in Chrome extension for the person's own signed-in browser, Chrome DevTools for parts the extension cannot reach.
- `client-asset-harvester`: Crawl a website and download every image (including JS-rendered, lazy-loaded and CSS background images) with metadata and logo detection.
- `deep-research`: Runs a structured multi-pass research process on any topic.
- `doctor`: Checks every tool and sign-in the workspace needs and re-installs only what is missing.
- `document-builder`: Turns notes or Markdown into a Word, PDF, PowerPoint, HTML or OpenDocument file, or a table into a spreadsheet-ready CSV.
- `explain-this`: Explains a contract, bill, medical letter, insurance statement, government notice or lease clause in plain words, with the dates and the three things to do.
- `gallery-dl`: Downloads original-size images from a social page or web gallery the person owns or has rights to.
- `gap-finder`: Notices when a task has no tool or skill, says so, and offers to build a skill, find an outside tool, or do it by hand once.
- `image-search`: Searches and downloads free stock photos from Pexels, Unsplash and Pixabay with the license recorded per image.
- `last30days`: Research what people actually say about any topic in the last 30 days.
- `loc-dl`: Downloads images from the Library of Congress with their rights statement attached.
- `markitdown`: Converts Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, HTML and Outlook files to Markdown with a provenance header.
- `media-tools`: Converts, trims, compresses and edits video, audio and images with ffmpeg, ImageMagick and four helpers: joins, speed changes, captions, watermarks, social reframes, fades.
- `meeting-prep`: Writes a one-page brief on a person or company before a call or meeting: who they are, what we have done together, what they want, three questions, one risk.
- `money-log`: Turns a bank or card CSV export into a monthly summary by category, a list of recurring charges, and an offline dashboard.
- `notebooklm-research`: Routing and judgment layer over the vendor notebooklm skill (Google Notebook): which NotebookLM artifact answers which question, how a YouTube link becomes usable research, notebook conventions, and the gates that keep generated output from being mistaken for finished work.
- `outbound-check`: Scans a draft or file for secrets, account numbers, other people's names and the owner's address before it is emailed, posted, uploaded or handed to someone.
- `paperwork`: Reads a scan, photo or PDF of a letter, bill, notice or form, names it by date, sender and type, files it, and pulls out the dates that matter.
- `photo-organizer`: Renames photos and videos by the date taken, sets exact duplicates aside and sorts the rest into year and month folders, after showing a plan.
- `resume-and-letter`: Tailors a resume to a job posting, writes the cover letter, or writes a formal letter with stakes (dispute, landlord, refund, complaint).
- `scrapling`: Reads public web pages that normal fetching refuses: 403 errors, pages that need JavaScript, empty results.
- `social-copywriter`: Writes social media posts and captions in the person's own voice for LinkedIn, X, Threads, Bluesky, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest and YouTube.
- `summarize`: Turns a document, folder, transcript or long thread into a short brief with decisions, open items and dates.
- `tool-vetting`: Vets tools from a link, video, post or list: finds each real repo, reads its install path and license, scans skills, names what does that job here today, returns ADOPT / EVALUATE / WATCH / MINE / ALREADY / SKIP.
- `trip-plan`: Turns pasted or filed booking confirmations into one itinerary page in date order, plus a list of what is still unbooked.
- `undo`: Keeps a save history of the workspace folder so any change can be taken back.
- `update`: Brings the workspace up to the latest Workspace Blueprint: new and fixed skills, new tools, the installer.
- `video-watcher`: Analyze video locally: no third-party video-analysis service.
- `voice-extractor`: Extracts a replicable voice profile from writing samples.
- `web-read`: Reads open web pages and searches the web with the built-in WebSearch and WebFetch tools, and files what it finds as a source extract.
- `weekly-brief`: Writes a one-page brief of the week: what came in, what went out, dates coming up, gaps logged, sign-ins needed.
- `workspace-audit`: Use when checking whether a workspace's map, router and notes still match the folders and files on disk.
- `workspace-diff`: Compares an outside source about how to work (a NotebookLM notebook, course, toolkit, blueprint) against this workspace and returns a claim-by-claim diff: ALREADY / GAP / CONFLICT / AHEAD / N/A, plus a scored, ranked change list.
- `writer`: Writes everyday messages in the person's own voice: emails, replies, notes, short documents, a thank-you, a complaint.
- `x-read`: Reads public X (Twitter) posts by URL, many at once, with links expanded.
