# Test: meeting-prep

## The ask
"Prep me for a call with Acme Plumbing tomorrow."

## The input
A pasted email thread with Acme Plumbing, and the workspace's own notes.

## Done looks like
One page with the five parts: who they are, what we have done together, what they want now, three questions, one risk. Every fact traces to the thread, the notes or a named web page. Nothing is invented, and a part with no source says "Nothing found".
