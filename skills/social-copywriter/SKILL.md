# social-copywriter

Posts and captions in the person's voice, shaped for each platform. The person posts it; this skill only drafts.

## When to use it

The person says "write a post", "caption this", "a tweet about", "an Instagram caption", "a pin description", "a video title", or shares a topic, a photo or a rough draft and wants it ready for a platform.

Route these elsewhere in one line, then stop:

- A message, email or note that is not for social media: `writer`.
- No `voice.md` yet and the person wants their style captured: `voice-extractor`.

## Steps

1. Read `voice.md`. If it does not exist, say so, offer `voice-extractor`, and write plain and direct in the meantime.
2. Ask only for what is missing, at most three questions: the one point (a topic, or what the photo or video shows), the platform or platforms, and the goal (saves, shares, replies, visits, sales, sign-ups). If the person already answered one, do not ask again. A topic plus a platform is enough to start.
3. Open `platforms.md` and find the row for each platform. Follow its length limit, first-line habit, hashtag count and link rule.
4. Draft one post per platform. Each platform gets its own wording; never paste one text across platforms. For a visual platform, the caption adds meaning to the picture or video and never just describes it.
5. Check each draft against the checklist below, then read it once as a stranger scrolling past would. Fix what fails.
6. Hand the drafts over in the chat, one under each platform name, and say where they are saved. Never post, schedule or send anything.

## Shape of every post

Three jobs, in this order:

1. The first line. It has to earn the tap on "more". See the hook patterns in `platforms.md`.
2. The payoff: the fact, story or tip the first line promised.
3. One next step, matched to the goal: a question, a save, a share, a visit, a reply.

Leave a blank line after every one or two lines. Never stack more than three lines without a break.

## Voice

Match `voice.md` first: word choice, sentence length, punctuation, emoji habits and mood. If it holds sample posts, copy their rhythm. If `voice.md` forbids a word or habit, that wins over everything in `platforms.md`.

## Checklist before handing over

- The first line would stop the person's own thumb.
- It sounds like them, not like a brand.
- One clear next step.
- Length and hashtag count match the platform's row.
- Links sit where the platform allows them (see the row).
- Every fact came from the person. Anything missing is a placeholder in square brackets, such as [date].
- No em dashes.

## Rules

- Use only the person's facts. Never invent a price, date, number, quote or result.
- Offer two or three versions with different first lines only when asked.
- For alt text on Instagram, write a short plain description of the image when the person wants one.
- A long thread, a slide-by-slide carousel or a content calendar is a bigger job: say so and ask whether to draft one post now.

## Never

- Post, schedule or send anything, on any channel.
- Put words in the person's mouth about a claim they did not make.
- Name or tag a third party's private matter without the person's yes.
