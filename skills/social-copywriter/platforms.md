# Platform rules for social-copywriter

Nine platforms, one row each. Limits and habits change over time; when the person knows a newer rule, theirs wins.

| Platform | Length | First-line habit | Hashtags | Links | What works |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 1,200 to 1,500 characters is the sweet spot; keep under 3,000 | A hook of one or two lines that earns the "see more" click. No warm-up. | 3 to 5, at the very end | None in the body (they cut reach); put the link in the first comment | A personal story, a specific number, first person, professional but not corporate. End with a question. |
| X | Under 280 characters | The hook is the post: bold, contrarian or a question | 0 to 2 | Allowed; keep the text short around it | Punchy and conversational. Cut every word that does not earn its place. |
| Threads | Up to 500 characters | A relatable opener, written like a text to a smart friend | 0 or 1 | Allowed | Human, a little raw, a real experience over advice. Polish reads as fake here. |
| Bluesky | Up to 300 characters | A dry observation or a plain confession | None | Allowed | Wit and a real point of view. Marketing language gets ignored. |
| Facebook | 40 to 80 characters for a photo post; 300 to 500 for a story | Line one carries the point (about 120 characters show on a phone) | 1 to 3, only if branded or local | Work fine in the body | Community moments, behind the scenes, local places, polls. Close with a direct question. Tag the Pages and people involved. |
| Instagram | Up to 2,200; photo 80 to 300, carousel 200 to 800, Reel 100 to 300 | The first 125 characters must work as a headline | 3 to 10, at the end or in the first comment | Not clickable in captions: say "link in bio" | A payoff the first line promises. Close on a save or share ask. Add alt text. |
| TikTok | Up to 2,200, but most strong ones stay under 150 | Echo or extend the hook the video already shows | 3 to 5: one broad, one niche, a few topical | Not clickable: say "link in bio" | Plain spoken, low polish. The caption is searched, so use words people would type. A curiosity gap that the video finishes. |
| Pinterest | Title up to 100 characters; description up to 500 | Keyword first, written like a headline a searcher would click | None (ignored); use natural keywords | In the link field, never in the text | Specific phrases such as "small kitchen ideas for renters". No emoji in the title. Say who it helps. |
| YouTube | Title 60 to 70 characters (100 max); description up to 5,000; Short caption under 150 | Title leads with the main keyword plus a number or a gap. The first 150 characters of the description are the hook. | Up to 3; add #shorts on a Short | Above the fold in the description, or in a pinned comment | Chapters with timestamps for any video over about three minutes. Never promise what the video does not deliver. |

## Hook patterns

Pick the one that fits the platform and the person's voice.

- Contrarian: say the opposite of what the reader expects, then prove it.
- Question: ask the thing the reader is already wondering.
- Story opener: start in the middle of a moment.
- Number: lead with one specific figure the person gave you.
- List preview: "3 things" with the real count.
- Confession: admit a mistake or a doubt.
- Before and after: show the change in one line.
- Empathy: name the feeling the reader has right now.

## Next step by goal

- Replies: an open question.
- Saves: "Save this for later."
- Shares: "Send this to someone who needs it."
- Visits or sales: one link or "link in bio", once.
- Follows: "Part 2 is coming" only if part 2 exists.
- Pinterest: no ask in the description; the link does the work.

## Visual posts

- Reel, TikTok or Short: the on-screen hook does the heavy lifting; the caption adds a punchline, a keyword or one ask.
- Carousel: the caption can run longer; hint at what the last slide reveals.
- Story: on-image text matters more than any caption.
- Pin: both title and description are filled, and both lead with the keyword.
- YouTube Community post: text first, like Facebook; polls and questions bring people back.

## Layout

Use either rhythm and stay consistent inside a post.

- One line, blank line, one line, blank line.
- One line, blank line, two lines, blank line, one line.

Do not reuse one hashtag set across platforms.
